My approach

Marketing is more than launching ads.

The real work is understanding the objective, following the customer journey, checking whether the data can be trusted and finding the next useful improvement.

A little more about me ↗

How I approach a problem

  • Start with the business goal, not the platform setting.
  • Map the customer journey and the key conversion actions.
  • Validate tracking before trusting campaign reports.
  • Use funnel KPIs to locate friction and prioritize changes.
  • Document what changed, what was learned and what comes next.
