About me

Curious about the numbers.
Focused on the outcome.

I’m Jayesh, a digital marketing professional developing practical capability across paid advertising, conversion tracking, analytics and campaign optimization.

I like understanding the “why” behind performance.

For me, digital marketing is not just about choosing an objective or launching an advertisement. It is about understanding the business goal, reaching the right audience, measuring the journey accurately and learning from the data.

My current focus is performance marketing: building a strong foundation in campaign planning, paid media, conversion measurement, funnel analysis and optimization.

I use practical projects and case studies to connect platform knowledge with real business questions—and to explain not only what I did, but why a particular decision matters.

My working mindset

Clear thinking, honest measurement and continuous improvement.

  • Understand the objective before selecting tactics.
  • Keep measurement definitions consistent.
  • Look beyond surface-level metrics.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Communicate findings in simple business language.
