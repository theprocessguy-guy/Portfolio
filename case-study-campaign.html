Featured case study 02 · Netzwerk Academy

Two months of testing, learning and optimization.

A full-funnel performance marketing case study for a digital marketing training and placement offer—moving from campaign foundations to audience and creative testing, lead-quality analysis and optimization decisions.

2-month campaignLead generationMeta AdsFunnel KPIsOptimization
01 · Business objective

Generate enquiries that can become enrolments

Netzwerk Academy offers digital marketing training with career and placement support. The campaign objective in this case study is to attract relevant prospective learners, generate enquiries or counselling-call bookings and understand which leads progress toward enrolment.

The important distinction is that a cheap lead is not automatically a good lead. Campaign evaluation must connect media performance with the quality and progression of enquiries.

02 · Funnel strategy

Plan campaigns around the decision journey

Awareness · Reach relevant prospects
Consideration · Explain value & fit
Lead · Capture genuine interest
Counselling · Qualify & follow up
Enrolment · Measure outcomes

The funnel is a planning model. The campaign setup should use the objective and conversion location that best match the available website journey and the business’s ability to follow up with leads.

03 · Month one

Build the foundation and test deliberately

Week 1 · Research & preparation

Understand the offer and audience

Review the course proposition, learner motivations, eligibility, objections and enquiry journey. Define the primary conversion action, campaign objective, tracking requirements and lead-quality feedback loop.

Week 2 · Campaign build

Set up the campaign structure

Organize campaigns and ad sets by funnel role or test variable. Prepare audience hypotheses, ad copy angles and creative variations. Check destination pages, forms, event tracking and UTM conventions before launch.

Weeks 3–4 · Controlled testing

Launch and collect learning

Test a limited number of audience and creative combinations. Monitor delivery, CPM, CTR, CPC, landing-page views and lead volume. Avoid making major decisions from very small or unstable samples.

04 · Month two

Optimize based on funnel evidence

Weeks 5–6 · Diagnose

Find where performance is leaking

Compare click-through, landing-page engagement and lead conversion. Review lead quality, contact rate and counselling-call attendance with the admissions or follow-up team. Identify whether the largest issue is creative, audience, landing page, form or follow-up.

Weeks 7–8 · Refine & consolidate

Shift effort toward stronger signals

Refresh weak creative, refine audience exclusions, improve the landing-page message match and prioritize combinations that generate qualified enquiries. Increase budgets cautiously only after performance and lead quality are stable.

05 · KPI framework

Measure each stage, not just the final lead count

CPMSpend ÷ impressions × 1,000
CTRClicks ÷ impressions × 100
CPCSpend ÷ clicks
LPV rateLanding-page views ÷ link clicks
CPLSpend ÷ leads
Lead-to-call rateCalls booked ÷ leads
Show-up rateCalls attended ÷ calls booked
Cost per enrolmentSpend ÷ enrolments
ROASAttributed revenue ÷ ad spend

Use consistent definitions and the same reporting window when comparing campaigns. Platform-reported attribution and CRM or admission records may differ, so reconcile them rather than assuming they will match exactly.

06 · Optimization decision guide

What the data should tell me to do next

  • Low CTR: review the audience-message match, hook, offer and creative relevance.
  • Clicks but fewer landing-page views: inspect page speed, redirects, mobile experience and tracking consistency.
  • Landing-page views but few leads: review promise-to-page alignment, form friction, trust signals and CTA clarity.
  • Leads but poor contact rate: check lead validity, response time, form questions and follow-up process.
  • Calls but few enrolments: review qualification, counselling quality, pricing clarity, objections and offer fit.
  • Stable qualified-lead performance: scale gradually while monitoring marginal cost and downstream quality.
07 · Reporting & learning

What a useful two-month report should contain

The final report should compare month one and month two across spend, reach, clicks, landing-page views, leads, qualified leads, calls, enrolments and cost per outcome. It should also document audience and creative tests, tracking limitations, what changed and why, and the next experiment.

No campaign spend, conversion totals, cost figures or revenue results have been added here because verified account data has not been provided. Insert actual results only after reconciling Ads Manager, website analytics and lead/enrolment records.
